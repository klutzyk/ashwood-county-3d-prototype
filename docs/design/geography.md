# Geographic Identity

Ashwood County is characterised by natural barriers that shape travel and settlement.

Roads follow valleys.

Towns develop beside rivers.

Forests dominate the northern highlands.

Farmland occupies the southern plains.

---

# Major Regions

## Northern Highlands

Features:

- Pine Ridge
- Fire Lookout
- Logging Camp
- Mountain roads
- Dense forests

Characteristics:

- High elevation
- Narrow roads
- Poor visibility
- Scenic overlooks

---

## Blackwater Basin

Features:

- Blackwater Lake
- Blackwater Dam
- Reservoir

Purpose:

Controls the county's primary water supply.

Acts as one of the county's most recognisable landmarks.

---

## Central Valley

Features:

- Ashwood
- River
- Highway 16

Characteristics:

Commercial and administrative centre.

Most residents lived here before the outbreak.

---

## Western Farmland

Features:

- Farm District
- Barns
- Silos
- Orchards
- Irrigation

Characteristics:

Open visibility.

Sparse population.

Long rural roads.

---

## Southern Plains

Features:

- County Fairgrounds
- Railway Crossing
- Mill Creek

Characteristics:

Lower elevation.

Agricultural land.

Transport corridor.

---

# Waterways

The Blackwater River begins at the dam and flows south through the county.

Important crossings include:

- Blackwater Dam
- Old Mill Bridge
- Railway Crossing

Water naturally divides the county into eastern and western regions.

---

# Forests

The State Forest occupies much of the northern county.

It contains:

- hiking trails
- logging roads
- abandoned camps
- wildlife
- remote cabins

The forest should feel easy to enter but difficult to fully understand.

---

# Roads

The primary transport route is Highway 16.

Secondary roads connect:

- Ashwood
- Mill Creek
- Pine Ridge
- Farm District

Numerous logging roads and dirt tracks extend into the forests.

---

# Travel Philosophy

Travel itself is gameplay.

Long stretches of road create anticipation.

Forests create uncertainty.

Towns provide temporary relief before presenting new dangers.

Every journey should feel meaningful.

---

# Landmarks

Major landmarks include:

- Blackwater Dam
- Fire Lookout
- Sheriff's Office
- Regional Hospital
- County Fairgrounds
- Blackwater Lake
- Old Mill Bridge

Each landmark should be visible from multiple locations whenever possible.

This helps players navigate naturally without relying on excessive UI.
