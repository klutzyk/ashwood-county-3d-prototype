# Main Street

> *"The spine of Historic Downtown. Once the busiest street in Ashwood, now silent except for the wind and the wandering dead."*

[← Back to Historic Downtown](../districts/historic_downtown.md)

---

# Overview

Main Street is the commercial heart of Ashwood County.

Before the outbreak, nearly every resident visited this street regularly.

It connected the Town Square, Sheriff's Office, local businesses, cafés, and community services into a compact, walkable district.

For many players, this will become the defining image of Ashwood.

---

# Design Goals

Main Street should:

- Become the visual icon of Ashwood.
- Encourage exploration of nearly every storefront.
- Support tense melee combat.
- Reward curiosity with meaningful interiors.
- Feel instantly recognisable from any screenshot.

# Layout

Main Street runs east-west through Historic Downtown.

Key features include:

- Wide sidewalks
- Parallel parking
- Decorative streetlights
- Crosswalks at major intersections
- Tree-lined footpaths
- Small laneways between buildings

The road should feel walkable rather than vehicle-focused.

# Streetscape

Street furniture should include:

- Wooden benches
- Bike racks
- Newspaper boxes
- Mailboxes
- Decorative planters
- Public rubbish bins
- Traffic lights
- Street signs
- Bus stop
- Fire hydrants

Everything should feel maintained before the outbreak.

---

# Architectural Style

Buildings should appear independently owned.

Characteristics:

- Brick façades
- Painted signage
- Large shopfront windows
- Fabric awnings
- Narrow frontages
- Decorative cornices

Avoid modern chain-store architecture.

---

# Gameplay

Main Street introduces the player to:

- Urban scavenging
- Close-quarters melee combat
- Window sightlines
- Alley navigation
- Building interiors

The street should naturally encourage players to move from shop to shop.

---

# Combat Design

Main Street is intentionally dangerous.

Combat spaces include:

- Parked vehicles
- Sidewalk cafés
- Shop entrances
- Narrow laneways
- Bus stop
- Outdoor seating

These create opportunities to:

- Funnel zombies
- Break line of sight
- Escape through buildings
- Circle around enemies

---

# Exploration

Players should discover:

- Locked back doors
- Rooftop access
- Hidden storage rooms
- Service alleys
- Basement entrances
- Rooftop maintenance ladders

Exploration should always feel worthwhile.

# Zombie Ecology

Typical population:

Medium to High.

Behaviour:

- Small wandering groups.
- Occasional larger clusters near intersections.
- Zombies often emerge from buildings after noise.

---

# Atmosphere

Main Street should evoke:

- familiarity
- quiet sadness
- abandonment
- tension
- curiosity

Players should imagine the street full of people only days earlier.

---

# Audio Design

Ambient sounds:

- Wind
- Rustling leaves
- Hanging signs creaking
- Distant birds
- Loose power lines humming
- Occasional zombie vocalisations

Music should be minimal.

Silence is more effective.

---

# Lighting

Preferred look:

Late afternoon.

Warm sunlight.

Long shadows.

Golden reflections on brick buildings.

Streetlights may flicker after dark but should not fully illuminate the road.

# Implementation Notes

Main Street should be the first fully realised street in Ashwood.

Its visual quality should establish the standard for every other urban environment in the game.

When players think of Ashwood County, Main Street should be the first place they remember.
