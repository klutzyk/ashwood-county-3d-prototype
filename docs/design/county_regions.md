# County Region Plan

The two concept maps are the authority for Ashwood County's macro layout. The
playable landmass is approximately 8.2 km east-west by 8.2 km north-south, with
Ashwood Main Street at world origin. Godot coordinates use +X east and -Z north.

## Shared County Foundation

- Blackwater River runs north to south through Blackwater Lake, the dam,
  Old Mill Bridge, and the southern canyon.
- State Highway 16 crosses the county west to east along Ashwood's southern edge.
- The freight railway enters from the southwest, passes the Mill Creek and
  fairgrounds corridor, then continues east.
- Paved county roads connect every settlement; gravel and dirt loops provide
  secondary routes through farms and forests.
- Pine Ridge and Fire Lookout are pinned to 1,210m and 1,380m respectively. The
  county never exceeds the planning map's 1,420m maximum.

## Regional Build Order

1. **Ashwood and Old Mill Bridge**: connect the existing authored town to its
   street grid, Highway 16, hospital, sheriff's office, and river crossing.
2. **Western Farm District**: establish field parcels, farm lanes, drainage,
   hedgerows, and the western highway approach.
3. **Blackwater Lake and Dam**: finish the reservoir shoreline, dam approaches,
   outflow gorge, and service roads.
4. **Pine Ridge Highlands**: shape the mountain settlement, logging camp, fire
   lookout, trail network, and winter-limited access.
5. **Mill Creek and Railway**: build the southwest settlement, creek crossings,
   freight facilities, and local street loop.
6. **Fairgrounds and Trailer Park**: establish the southeastern residential and
   event corridor linked to Highway 16 and the railway.
7. **South Farmland**: create broad rolling agricultural land, farmsteads, and
   the southern county road loop.
8. **Eastern Woodlands**: complete the forest road loops, vistas, and eastern
   Highway 16 exit toward Cedar Falls.

Detailed buildings, props, loot, encounters, and final vegetation are separate
regional passes. Roads, water, terrain, and region anchors remain shared county
data so later work cannot drift away from the concept layout.
